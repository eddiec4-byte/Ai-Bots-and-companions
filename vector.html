Vector — Review

AI robot with a face, voice, and real autonomy — a classic smart-desk companion. Made by Anki / Digital Dream Labs.

Our review

Vector is the little robot with a big persona — it recognizes faces, navigates your desk, and projects real autonomy that still feels magical years on. Digital Dream Labs revived it after Anki, and the community keeps it alive. The catch is that some cloud smarts sit behind a subscription and support has been uneven. For tinkerers and anyone who wants a genuinely 'alive' desk buddy rather than a static pet, Vector is a cult favorite for good reason.

Pros

  • Charming, capable personality
  • Hackable / dev-friendly

Cons
