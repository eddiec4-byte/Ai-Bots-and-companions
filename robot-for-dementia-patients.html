❓ Frequently Asked

Can a robot really help someone with dementia?

Robots like ElliQ offer routine reminders, familiar conversation, and consistent company that can reduce sundowning anxiety and missed meds. They do not replace carers, but they add a steady presence between visits.

Which robot is safest for a dementia patient?

ElliQ is purpose-built for seniors with a simple, non-intrusive interface. Avoid small parts or anything that could be a trip hazard. Always supervise the first weeks.

Do these robots require Wi-Fi and setup help?

Yes, both ElliQ and Lovot need Wi-Fi and an initial setup, which a family member or carer should do. Ongoing use is designed to be hands-off for the patient.
