Aibo — Review

Sony's robot dog (ERS-1000) — the longest-running consumer companion robot, with a live US store at us.aibo.com. Made by Sony.

Our review

Aibo is the elder statesman of companion robots. Sony has iterated on the robot dog since the late 1990s, and the current ERS-1000 generation is the most convincing four-legged companion you can actually buy — it walks, reacts to touch and voice, and develops behaviour patterns over time. It is also the most expensive option on this list by a wide margin, and Sony layers cloud services on top. If you want a companion with a real manufacturer behind it and a decade-plus track record rather than a startup gamble, Aibo is the safest name in the category. Official store verified live this run at us.aibo.com.
