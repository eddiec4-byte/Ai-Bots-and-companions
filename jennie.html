Jennie — Review

Our review

Jennie is Tombot's robotic Labrador puppy, built from the ground up as an emotional-support animal rather than a gadget. It is designed for people living with dementia and others for whom a live dog is not practical: it responds to touch and voice with puppy-like movement and sound, needs no feeding or walking, and carries no bite or trip risk. Tombot sells direct through its own store, which was verified live on 2026-08-06, and demand is managed through a waitlist rather than instant shipping. If your goal is calm, tactile comfort for a relative in care rather than a talkative desk robot, Jennie is the most narrowly and seriously targeted product in this category.
