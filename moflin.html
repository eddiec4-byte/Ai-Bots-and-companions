Moflin — Review

Artificial-intelligence pet with lifelike fur and evolving 'emotions'. Made by Casio.

Our review

Moflin is Casio's bet that a robot can feel like a living thing. Its soft fur shifts and its 'mood' appears to evolve the more you interact — a genuinely soothing, almost meditative object to have nearby. It's less a tool than a comfort object, and that's the point. The trade-off is price and the fact it doesn't *do* much beyond being present. If you want a calm, beautiful companion rather than a task-doer, Moflin is one of the most polished options out there.
